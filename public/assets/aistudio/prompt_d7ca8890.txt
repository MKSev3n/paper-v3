## 2. PROJECT VISION

Paper should be a fully functional mobile novel reader inspired by the functionality of command-line novel crawlers and downloaders, but with a polished graphical interface.

The application should allow users to:

1. Discover novels from supported online sources.
2. Search for novels using their titles and keywords.
3. Retrieve novel metadata, descriptions, authors, covers, and chapter lists.
4. Download individual chapters or entire novels.
5. Monitor download progress.
6. Save downloaded novels on the device.
7. Read downloaded chapters without internet access.
8. Continue reading from the last saved position.
9. Bookmark chapters and manage reading preferences.
10. Export downloaded novels as EPUB where supported.

The goal is to make Paper a complete, offline-capable novel reading application.
